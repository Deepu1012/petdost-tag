PetDost Privacy Policy

Applies to the PetDost app, the PetDost Partner app and the PetDost QR tag page. Last updated: 11 November 2026.

हिंदी में सार: PetDost आपकी जानकारी केवल ऐप चलाने के लिए इस्तेमाल करता है: आपके पशुओं का रिकॉर्ड रखना, आरक्षण करवाना और सूचनाएँ भेजना। हम आपकी जानकारी बेचते नहीं हैं और विज्ञापन नहीं दिखाते। आप कभी भी ऐप में "खाता हटाएँ" से अपना खाता और सारी जानकारी हटा सकते हैं।

1. Who we are

PetDost ("we") runs a marketplace in India that helps pet owners keep their pets' health records and book vets, groomers and pet shops. Contact: petdost.support@gmail.com.

Your app PIN is stored only on your phone, in scrambled (hashed) form. Fingerprint unlock is handled by Android; your fingerprint never reaches PetDost.

3. What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't read your location in the background; only when you tap "Use my current location".

4. Services we use

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in and photo storage.
  • Google: Google sign-in, Firebase Cloud Messaging (notifications) and Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports).

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access in our database is limited per account, so people only see their own data and what's shared with them as described above.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, it is deleted as described on our account deletion page. Crash reports are kept by Firebase for up to 90 days.

6. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can see and correct your data in the app, delete your account at any time, and contact us with any question or complaint at petdost.support@gmail.com. Grievance officer: Deepanshu Vasistha, petdost.support@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

7. Children

PetDost is meant for adults (18+). We don't knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app for important changes.
