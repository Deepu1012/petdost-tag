2. What PetDost does — and doesn't

PetDost is an intermediary. We list businesses, take your booking and pass it on. We do not provide veterinary treatment, grooming, boarding or goods ourselves, and we do not employ the people who do.

The service you book is a contract between you and that business. The business is responsible for its own licences, qualifications, prices, the quality and safety of what it does, and for following the law.

We check listings in good faith but cannot guarantee that every detail — prices, timings, services, qualifications — is accurate or current. Ratings and reviews are written by other users and are their opinions, not ours.

3. PetDost is not a vet

Nothing in the app is veterinary advice. Reminders, vaccine schedules, record keeping and any information shown are for your convenience only and are not a substitute for an examination by a qualified veterinarian.

In an emergency, or if your pet seems unwell, contact a vet immediately. Do not wait for a booking through the app.

Vaccination intervals and due dates shown in the app are general defaults. Your vet's advice always takes priority.

5. Bookings, visits and cancellation

  • A booking request is confirmed only when the business accepts it. A business may decline or, occasionally, cancel.
  • Please cancel in the app as early as you can if your plans change. Repeatedly not turning up may lead us to limit your account.
  • For a home visit, give a correct address and make sure someone is there to receive the vet or groomer.
  • When a visit is finished and paid for, you give the business a 4-digit visit code. This confirms the visit happened and lets you rate it. Don't share the code before the visit is complete.
  • If something goes wrong with a visit code, either side can raise a dispute and PetDost will decide it fairly based on what both sides say. That decision is about the record in the app only.

Prices in the app are what the business has entered. Final charges can differ if the actual treatment or service differs — the business should tell you before doing extra work.

If we introduce in-app payment or fees for businesses later, we will say so clearly in the app before it applies to you.

You can rate and review a business only after a completed visit. Keep reviews honest and about your own experience. We may remove a review that is abusive, false, contains personal details of other people, or is posted to manipulate ratings.

  • Honour bookings you accept, and respond to booking requests promptly.
  • Treat animals humanely and customers respectfully. Use customer details (name, phone, address) only to carry out that booking, never for marketing or any other purpose, and keep them confidential.
  • Don't enter a visit code you were not given by the customer.

9. QR tags and lost pets

A PetDost QR tag opens a page where a finder can contact you. It is a help, not a tracking device: it works only if someone finds your pet and scans it, and it shows whatever contact details you chose to make visible. Please keep those details current. We can't guarantee a lost pet will be found.
