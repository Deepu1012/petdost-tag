PetDost खाता हटाएँ · Delete your PetDost account

ऐप से (सबसे आसान)

  1. PetDost या PetDost Partner ऐप खोलें।
  2. ऊपर दाईं ओर खाते के चिह्न को दबाएँ।
  3. "खाता हटाएँ" चुनें और पुष्टि करें।

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open PetDost or PetDost Partner.
  2. Tap the account icon at the top right.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm.

Can't open the app? · ऐप नहीं खुल रहा?

Email petdost.support@gmail.com from the Gmail address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my PetDost account". We delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

जिस Gmail से आप प्रवेश करते हैं, उससे petdost.support@gmail.com पर "Delete my PetDost account" विषय के साथ ईमेल करें। हम 7 दिन में खाता हटाकर ईमेल से बताएँगे।

What is deleted · क्या हटता है

Your account works in both apps, so deleting it removes it from both, permanently:

  • Your profile, sign-in, phone number, saved addresses and language
  • Your pets, photos and all their records (vet visits, vaccines, food, grooming)
  • QR tags (they stop working) and reports from finders
  • Your bookings and reviews. Upcoming bookings are cancelled first and the business is told.
  • Any business you listed in PetDost Partner, with its services and photos. Customers with upcoming visits are told.
  • Your notification registrations

What is kept · क्या रहता है

  • Customers who visited a business you listed keep their own past booking history (with the business name), without any link to your account.
  • Crash reports, which contain no account details, are deleted automatically by Firebase within 90 days.
